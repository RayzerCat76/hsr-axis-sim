Timeline rules:
1. The next normal actor is the alive unit with the lowest current_av.
2. When selecting a normal actor, advance global_av by min(current_av), then subtract that value from all alive normal timeline units.
3. After a normal turn ends, add base_av to the actor's current_av.
4. Extra turns must be resolved before the normal timeline.
5. Extra turns must not alter the unit's original normal timeline position.
6. Immediate action sets current_av to 0.
7. Action advance uses:
   new_current_av = max(0, current_av - base_av * percent)
8. Action delay uses:
   new_current_av = current_av + base_av * percent
9. Speed change uses:
   new_current_av = old_current_av * old_speed / new_speed
   then update speed and base_av = 10000 / new_speed
10. DoesNotEndTurn sets turn_context.should_end_turn = False.
    It must not create an extra turn and must not reset the actor's AV yet.
