Representative character specs to add:

1. `kill_chain_carry_mvp.json`
   A Seele-like single-target carry.
   Required mechanics:
   - basic attack: single enemy, gains SP, gains energy, does damage, does toughness damage
   - skill: single enemy, costs SP, gains energy, does stronger damage, does toughness damage
   - ultimate: single enemy, consumes energy, does damage, does toughness damage, can be used in interrupt window
   - trigger: on unit_defeated by this unit, grant this unit an extra turn
   - Must use the generic trigger system, not hard-coded character logic.

2. `turn_pull_support_mvp.json`
   A Bronya-like turn-pull support.
   Required mechanics:
   - basic attack: single enemy, gains SP and energy
   - skill: single other ally, costs SP, immediately advances / immediately actions that ally, applies a current-turn or target-normal-turn damage buff
   - ultimate: all allies, consumes energy, applies an offensive buff
   - Must use generic effects such as AddBuff, ImmediateAction, AdvanceAction, ConsumeEnergy, GainEnergy, etc.

3. `energy_battery_support_mvp.json`
   A Tingyun-like energy and attack support.
   Required mechanics:
   - skill: single other ally, applies an attack buff
   - ultimate: single ally, consumes energy, grants energy and a damage buff
   - basic attack: single enemy, gains SP and energy
   - Must validate target legality for ally-targeted skills.

4. `break_support_mvp.json`
   A Ruan Mei-like break support MVP.
   Required mechanics:
   - skill: all allies or selected ally, applies break-effect-related buff(s)
   - ultimate: all allies or enemies, applies break damage bonus / resistance penetration / vulnerability style modifier using existing stat_mods where possible
   - basic attack: single enemy, gains SP and energy
   - If the current engine lacks a clean way to represent toughness damage efficiency / break efficiency, add a minimal stat pipeline for it:
     - support a numeric stat mod such as `toughness_damage_bonus` or `break_efficiency`
     - `DealToughnessDamage` should multiply the base toughness damage by `(1 + relevant_bonus)` from the attacker’s effective stats
     - Keep this isolated and tested
   - Do not implement the exact official character.
