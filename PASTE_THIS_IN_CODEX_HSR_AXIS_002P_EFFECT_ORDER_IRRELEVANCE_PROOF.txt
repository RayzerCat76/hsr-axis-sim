Objective
=========

Determine whether the two candidate effect orders are observably equivalent under the COMPLETE CURRENT SIMULATOR CONTRACT:

A. target Energy restoration, then DMG-buff application;
B. DMG-buff application, then target Energy restoration.

This is a current-engine proof only. Release-game order must remain unknown.

The task may conclude either:

- `proven_irrelevant_under_current_simulator_contract`; or
- `not_proven_irrelevant_under_current_simulator_contract` with exact blockers.

Do not force a positive conclusion if any current observable differs.

Required synthetic comparison harness
=====================================

Create a test/audit-only harness that executes the two candidate orders in isolated deep-equivalent states. Do not modify the existing Tingyun reviewed binding and do not register a new handler.

Use a synthetic non-ending interrupt-style action and the existing `GainEnergy` and `AddBuff` primitives. The synthetic buff may use an unmistakably non-real ID such as `synthetic_tingyun_order_probe`; it must never enter the reviewed registry or a real character spec.

Compare complete canonical observables after execution, not merely target Energy and buff presence.

At minimum include:

1. target Energy below cap;
2. target Energy near cap, exercising clamp behavior;
3. target Energy already at cap;
4. no existing probe buff;
5. existing same-ID probe buff with deterministic refresh;
6. unrelated existing buffs and debuffs;
7. multiple non-target units with nontrivial state;
8. `action_started` and `action_finished` triggers, including trigger effects, to prove that trigger boundaries do not expose intermediate order;
9. nontrivial logs, pending events, trigger counters, SP, AV, HP, toughness, extra-turn stack, and enemy-AI state;
10. interrupt `TurnContext` preservation and no turn end.

Canonical comparison must cover at least:

- every `Unit` field;
- buff/debuff values and metadata;
- Energy and caps;
- global and unit AV;
- HP/toughness/alive state;
- skill points;
- extra-turn stack;
- logs;
- pending event sequence and event data;
- trigger fire counts;
- event dispatch count;
- enemy AI plans and cursors;
- every `TurnContext` field;
- action result/return shape.

If any field must be excluded, document the exact reason and prove that it is not user-visible or contract-visible. Prefer no exclusions.
